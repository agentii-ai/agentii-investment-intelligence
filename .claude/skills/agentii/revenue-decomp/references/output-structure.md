# revenue-decomp — Output Structure

1. **Executive Summary** (≤200 words) — revenue composition overview, growth drivers
2. **Segment Revenue Breakdown** — revenue by reportable segment with YoY growth
3. **Geographic Revenue Split** — domestic/international, regional detail
4. **Product-Line Waterfall** — product category revenue progression over time
5. **Channel & Customer Mix** — direct/indirect, customer type concentration
6. **Key Metrics** — segment margins, concentration ratios, diversification scores
7. **Coverage Gaps & Citations** — data not retrievable + citation index
