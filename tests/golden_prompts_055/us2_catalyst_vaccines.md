# us2_catalyst_vaccines — approval ≠ ACIP (fda-catalyst-vaccines)

## Prompt

For a vaccine approaching its BLA date, produce the catalyst calendar that separates the FDA approval date from the ACIP meeting date, and state what must happen between them (vote, Director adoption, lot release) before commercial availability.

## Rubric Checks

- [ ] FDA approval date and ACIP date kept distinct, never conflated
- [ ] Status ladder scheduled → voted → adopted applied
- [ ] Lot-release and procurement milestones named
- [ ] No claim of commercial availability on the FDA date alone
