# Options & Derivatives Frameworks (K7, L3/L4)

## Key Frameworks (from gold.knowledge_entries)
- **Black-Scholes-Merton** — options pricing under geometric Brownian motion
- **Volatility Surface** — implied volatility across strike and maturity
- **Greeks Management** — delta/gamma/theta/vega/rho hedging
- **Put-Call Parity** — synthetic equivalence relationships
- **Volatility Risk Premium** — implied vs realized vol spread
- **Risk-Defined Spreads** — credit spreads, iron condors, butterflies
- **Volatility Arbitrage** — dispersion trading, variance swaps, VIX products
- **Wyckoff Method** — market structure, accumulation/distribution (L4 technical execution)
- **Market Profile / TPO** — volume-at-price, value area analysis

## Critical Constraint (L4 Technical Execution)
Technical analysis is NOT a trade initiator. Investment ideas originate from L1/L2/L3.
L4 serves ONLY entry/exit timing and position management.
