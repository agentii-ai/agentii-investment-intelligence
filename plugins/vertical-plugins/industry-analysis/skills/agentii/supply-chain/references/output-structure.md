# supply-chain — Output Structure

1. **Executive Summary** (≤200 words) — supply-chain structure, key dependencies, risk level
2. **Supplier Map** — key suppliers, concentration, geographic distribution
3. **Customer Concentration** — top customers, revenue dependency, contract terms
4. **Geographic Exposure** — manufacturing, sourcing, and distribution by region
5. **Bottleneck Analysis** — single points of failure, lead-time risks, inventory buffers
6. **Risk Assessment** — tariff exposure, geopolitical risks, logistics vulnerabilities
7. **Key Metrics** — supplier/customer concentration ratios, inventory turnover, DIO
8. **Coverage Gaps & Citations** — data not retrievable + citation index

**Multi-ticker output**: write to `_cross/{descriptive-slug}_{YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM}_supply-chain_{affix}.md`
(frontmatter `tickers: [...]`). Pure industry/thematic analyses use
`_sector/{sector_name}/{YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM}_supply-chain_{affix}.md`.
