## Multi-Source Synthesis

Multiple established stock selection methodologies converge on a common set of screening factors despite different naming conventions and emphasis. This reference presents the synthesized institutional consensus — the factors where multiple independent methodologies agree — while preserving source-specific contributions where institutions genuinely differ.

---

## Synthesized Screening Factors (Cross-Source Consensus)

### Factor 1: Earnings Growth (CANSLIM C+A ≈ MAGNET A ≈ QUEST E)

**Consensus Threshold**: Minimum 18-25% year-over-year quarterly EPS growth.

**Why It Matters**: Earnings are the primary fundamental driver of stock prices. Companies with accelerating quarterly earnings signal business momentum, competitive advantages, and management execution. Historical analysis of top-performing stocks shows the majority exhibited EPS growth of at least 70% in the quarter before their major price advance began.

**Scoring (0-100)**:
- ≥ 50% YoY growth: 100 points
- 25-50%: 75 points
- 18-25%: 50 points
- 10-18%: 25 points
- < 10%: 0 points

**Annual Earnings Growth**: Supplement with 3-5 year annual EPS growth ≥ 25% and ROE ≥ 17%. Consistency of growth matters more than magnitude — a 12% compounder over 5 years is higher quality than a 30% grower with high variance.

### Factor 2: Revenue Growth (MAGNET Primary)

**Consensus Threshold**: Revenue growth ≥ 15% year-over-year.

**Why It Matters**: Revenue growth is harder to manipulate than earnings growth. Earnings can be managed through accounting choices; revenue is what it is. Companies growing revenue at ≥ 15% are typically gaining market share or operating in expanding markets.

### Factor 3: Quality (QUEST Q, CANSLIM A, MAGNET Profitability)

**Consensus Threshold**: ROE ≥ 17%, durable competitive moat.

Quality screens for business models that can sustain high returns. ROE alone is insufficient — it must be decomposed into DuPont components to confirm the source (margin, turnover, or leverage). High ROE from leverage is not quality.

### Factor 4: Relative Strength / Momentum (CANSLIM L ≈ MAGNET M)

**Consensus Threshold**: Relative Strength Rating ≥ 80 (top 20% of all stocks), top 1-2 in industry group.

**Why It Matters**: Stocks that have already demonstrated price strength tend to continue demonstrating it in the intermediate term. The market is a voting machine in the short run — relative strength tells you what the market is voting for.

**RS Calculation**: Weighted average of price change over 12 months (with heavier weight on recent 3 months) vs. all other stocks.

### Factor 5: Supply & Demand (CANSLIM S)

**Consensus Threshold**: Low float preferred. Volume surge on breakout ≥ 50% above average daily volume.

**Why It Matters**: Institutional-quality stocks have sufficient float for liquidity but tight enough supply that buying pressure moves price. Volume confirmation on breakout is the single most reliable technical validation of a fundamental signal — it means institutions are acting on the same thesis.

### Factor 6: Institutional Sponsorship (CANSLIM I)

**Consensus Threshold**: 3-10 quality institutional owners who are accumulating (not selling). At least one with above-average fund performance.

**Why It Matters**: Institutional ownership validates the thesis with professional due diligence. But too many institutional owners (> 20) means the stock is widely discovered — limited upside remaining. The sweet spot is 3-10 funds that are actively building positions.

### Factor 7: Market Direction (CANSLIM M)

**Consensus Threshold**: Only initiate positions in confirmed market uptrends.

**Why It Matters**: Three out of four stocks follow the general market direction. A perfect fundamental setup in a bear market has substantially lower probability of working. The market direction filter is binary: confirmed uptrend → screen actively; correction or bear market → screen for watchlist only, do not deploy capital.

## MAGNET Sell Discipline

- **Holding Period**: 6-9 months typical (turnover > 100% annually)
- **15% Decline Rule**: Investigate any position down 15%. Determine whether the thesis is intact or broken.
- **20%+ Decline**: Sell regardless — something is wrong that analysis missed.
- **Fundamental Deterioration OR Technical Deterioration**: Either is sufficient reason to exit. Waiting for both to confirm means exiting at the worst possible price.
- **Tax Considerations**: Secondary to investment discipline. Staying in a declining position to avoid taxes converts a small loss into a large one.

## Screen Construction Best Practices

1. **Apply market direction filter FIRST**: No screen is reliable in a bear market. Confirmed uptrend is the prerequisite.
2. **Screen within leading industry groups**: Industry group RS ranking is as important as individual stock RS. Screen in the top 20-30 industry groups.
3. **Volume confirmation on every signal**: A fundamental screen hit without volume confirmation is noise. Volume tells you institutions agree.
4. **Position size by conviction**: Higher composite score → larger position. But never exceed single-position risk limits (default 5%).
5. **Review screens at least weekly**: A stock that qualified last week may not qualify this week. Screening is a continuous process, not a one-time filter.
