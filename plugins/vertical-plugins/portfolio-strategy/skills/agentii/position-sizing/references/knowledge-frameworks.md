# L3 Portfolio Strategy Frameworks

## Key Frameworks (from gold.knowledge_entries)
- **Kelly Criterion** — optimal position sizing based on edge and odds
- **Black-Litterman Model** — Bayesian portfolio construction with investor views
- **Risk Budgeting** — allocation by risk contribution, not capital weight
- **Grinold-Kahn Fundamental Law** — IR = IC × √(breadth)
- **Fama-French Factor Models** — value, size, momentum, quality, low-volatility factors
- **VaR / CVaR** — tail-risk measurement and scenario stress-testing
