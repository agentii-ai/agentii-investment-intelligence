# ct-status-diff — Knowledge Frameworks

Original paraphrase of publicly taught clinical-trial monitoring discipline. No external research text is quoted.

## The 11-Category Change Taxonomy

- **New** — no prior snapshot row; the trial entered the registry view.
- **Suspended / Withdrawn / Terminated** — regulatory or sponsor stops; Terminated carries the highest risk weight.
- **Recruiting / Recruitment-Complete / Completed** — the enrollment lifecycle; Recruitment-Complete starts the readout clock.
- **Ahead / Delayed** — primary-completion re-timing; the earliest signal of program momentum.
- **Upsized / Downsized** — enrollment-count moves; Downsized is the earliest visible distress indicator (a collapse in planned enrollment often precedes a termination).

## The Stored-Previous-Value Discipline

The diff is only meaningful because the prior week's state was STORED before this week's arrived. Every reported change pairs new with previous; a change without a previous value is an inference, and inferences are never presented as facts. Gap windows (missing snapshot weeks) are stated verbatim on the rows they affect — a change is never attributed across a gap.

## Signal Weighting

1. **Risk signals** (Terminated, Downsized): portfolio-level red flags — check the sponsor's pipeline dependence on the asset, the safety read-through to the mechanism, and the cash-runway implication.
2. **Re-timing alerts** (Ahead, Delayed): program-momentum signals — a Delayed pivotal date pushes the whole catalyst calendar; an Ahead date pulls it forward.
3. **Flow changes** (New, Recruiting, Recruitment-Complete, Completed): the readout calendar's heartbeat.

## Readout-Clock Rule

Recruitment-Complete is the reliable "readout clock started" marker — pair it with the primary-completion date to date the catalyst. An unchanged status between snapshots is information too: a trial that was due to complete and did not is a silent delay.

## Badge Discipline

- `[FACT]` — registry rows and their previous/new values.
- `[DEDUCTED]` — signal interpretations (e.g., enrollment collapse implies distress).
- `[VIEW]` — positioning judgments; always labeled, never conflated with facts.
