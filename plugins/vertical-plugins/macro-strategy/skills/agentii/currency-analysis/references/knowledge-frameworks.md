# L1 Macro Strategy Frameworks

## Key Frameworks (from gold.knowledge_entries)
- **Dalio Debt Cycle Model** — short-term (5-8 year) and long-term (50-75 year) debt cycles
- **Minsky Financial Instability Hypothesis** — stability breeds instability, three phases of credit
- **Kindleberger Bubble Stage Model** — displacement, boom, euphoria, distress, revulsion
- **Yield Curve Forecasting** — 2s10s spread, 3m10y spread, inversion as recession signal
- **Taylor Rule** — central bank rate-setting response to inflation gap and output gap
- **Kondratiev Waves** — 40-60 year long-cycle technological innovation waves
